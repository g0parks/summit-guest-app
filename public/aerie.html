The gap you already know

After your guests effortlessly book their dream vacation home, they are left with a variety of tasks to build out the rest of their vacation. Ski gear, transportation, a private chef, or lift tickets procured and delivered to their residence. Even with a dedicated concierge desk, you can't help everyone.

Aerie changes that. From the moment of booking (along with information your reservations team collects), Aerie delivers a custom welcome flow that allows them to get everything they need for their stay in just a few minutes.

What Aerie is

Aerie is that concierge, run for you and presented as yours.

Your guests get one point of contact for the non-lodging parts of their trip. Summit operates it end to end, under your name and to the highest white-glove standards. You add nothing to your headcount and pay nothing to run it.

How it works

The guest pays for what they use, at rates no one can get alone.

Your guest opens the link in your brand and makes their requests. Summit fulfills each one through our Park City vendor network. Because we buy across dozens of guests per week, we secure rates a single firm can't.

Nothing to spin up, no one to hire. You add Aerie to your pre-arrival flow and we run the rest. A full concierge you offer your guests at no cost to your business.

A note on Airbnb

In 2025 Airbnb launched Services. The platform now offers a concierge layer too.

Chefs, gear, and local services are a tap away inside the app. A guest deciding between booking direct with you and booking on the platform leans toward whoever makes the stay effortless. Aerie puts that concierge on your side of the choice, so the booking stays with you.
