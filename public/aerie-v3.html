How it works for you

Free to you, and not the kind of free with a catch.

The guest pays for what they use. You never see a bill, a setup fee, or a rev-share line. Here is the actual math: because Aerie buys across many homes, it secures rates a single manager can't get alone, and our margin is the gap between those rates and what the guest pays. We earn from the buying, not from you. Nothing to install, nobody to hire.

You already made booking the home effortless. This makes the rest of the trip effortless too, and it shows up under your name.

The long game

A better stay is the reason a guest comes back, and comes back direct.

The stay people remember is the stay they rebook. You already built a brand worth returning to. Aerie hands the guest one more reason to skip the platform next time and come straight to you. That direct booking, off Airbnb and its fees, is the most profitable one on your books.

A note on Airbnb

In 2025 Airbnb launched Services. The concierge layer is theirs now too.

Chefs, gear, the local extras, all one tap deep inside their app. So the next time a guest decides between booking you direct or booking through Airbnb, the deciding factor is who makes the stay easier. Without Aerie, that is them. With it, that is you.
