How it works for you

The operators you'd choose anyway, at rates no single manager could ask for alone.

Aerie books across many homes, so it secures rates a lean firm can't get on its own. Aerie's margin is the gap between those rates and what the guest pays. None of it is a charge to you. There is nothing to install, no staff to add, and no fee at any point.

The long game

A better stay is what brings a guest back to book direct.

A guest who had an effortless stay remembers where they had it. They book again, and the booking that comes straight to you, off the platforms and their fees, is the most profitable one you take. You already built a brand worth returning to. Aerie hands the guest one more reason to come back to it.

A note on Airbnb

In 2025 Airbnb launched Services. The platform now runs a concierge layer of its own.

Chefs, gear and local experiences are a tap away inside their app. When a guest weighs a direct booking with you against booking in the app, they lean toward whichever one makes the stay effortless. Aerie puts that concierge on your side of the choice, so the booking stays with you.
