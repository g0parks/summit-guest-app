What it costs you

You pay nothing. Not a fee, not a seat, not a cut of your nightly rate.

The guest pays for what they use. We make our margin the way a wholesaler does, on the gap between the rates we secure and the price the guest pays. Because we buy these services across many homes, we get numbers one manager booking for a few houses never could. That spread is ours. Your books are untouched.

There is nothing to install and no one to hire. You hand a guest the link, and a full concierge operation runs under your name from that point on. It is the one upgrade to your service that adds to your offer and subtracts from no margin you keep.
