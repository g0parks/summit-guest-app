The gap you already know

Your guests arrive with a list. A car waiting at the airport. Groceries in the fridge. Ski gear fitted before the first morning. A chef booked for the night they don't feel like cooking. Right now that list either lands on your team, or it doesn't get handled and shows up in the review.

Hiring a concierge desk to cover it is real payroll for a service that doesn't bill. So most managers leave it to the guest, and the stay falls a little short. The work is valuable. Carrying it yourself rarely pencils out.

What Aerie is

Aerie is that concierge, run for you and presented as yours.

Your guests get a single point of contact for the parts of their trip. Summit operates it end to end. You add nothing to your headcount and pay nothing to run it. It is not software you administer or a tool dropped into your stack. It is a service that lives under your name, staffed and fulfilled by Summit, accountable to your standards.

What it does for you

  • 01

    Better stays, on the record

    The requests that used to slip get handled. That shows up where it counts for you, in reviews and in what owners hear about how their home is run.

  • 02

    More repeat and direct bookings

    A guest whose trip was handled comes back to you, and books with you directly next time instead of through a platform.

  • 03

    Zero overhead, zero cost

    No concierge to hire, no system to stand up, no per-stay fee. Aerie costs you nothing to operate. Guests pay only for what they choose to use.

Your guest. Your brand. Your relationship.

You spent years building a name owners trust and guests recognize. Aerie runs entirely inside it.

Guests see you, not Summit. The concierge that books their car and fits their gear is yours as far as they know, and the goodwill comes back to you.

How it works, briefly

  • 01

    Your guest gets a link in your brand

    Before arrival they open it and arrange the parts of their stay: a car from the airport, groceries, fitted ski or bike gear, experiences, and most anything else they ask for.

  • 02

    Summit fulfills it

    Every request runs through our vendor network. Because we buy across many homes, we secure rates a single manager couldn't, and the guest pays for what they use.

  • 03

    You do nothing and pay nothing

    No setup work on your end, no invoices, no management. The stay just runs better.

Our margin is the difference between the rates we secure and what the guest pays. That is the whole model, and it is why running it costs you nothing.
