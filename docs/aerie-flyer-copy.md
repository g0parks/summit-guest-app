## Section 1 — The problem
- **Eyebrow:** The gap you already know
- **Body (paragraph 1):** Your guests arrive with a list. A car at the airport. Groceries in the fridge. Ski gear fitted before the first morning. A chef for the night they don't feel like cooking. Right now that list either lands on your team, or it doesn't get handled and turns up in the review.
- **Body (paragraph 2):** Hiring a concierge desk to cover it is real payroll for a service that doesn't bill. So most managers leave it to the guest, and the stay falls a little short. The work is worth doing. Carrying it yourself rarely pencils out.

## Section 2 — What Aerie is
- **Eyebrow:** What Aerie is
- **Lede (big line):** Aerie is that concierge, run for you and presented as yours.
- **Body:** Your guests get one point of contact for the parts of their trip. Summit operates it end to end, under your name and to your standards. You add nothing to your headcount and pay nothing to run it.

### The five service categories (title + one-line subtitle each)
1. **Transport** — A driver meets them at the airport and brings them to the home.
2. **Grocery Stocking** — The guest sends a list. The kitchen is stocked and put away before they arrive.
3. **Equipment Rentals** — Skis and bikes sized to each guest, delivered and fitted at the home.
4. **Experiences** — A private chef, a mountain guide, an in-home massage, booked and confirmed.
5. **More** — A crib for the nursery, a tee time at dawn, ski lockers, a florist, oxygen for the altitude. If a Park City vendor can do it, the guest can ask.

## Section 3 — How it works for you (the green band)
- **Eyebrow:** How it works for you
- **Headline:** Free to you. The guest pays for what they use, at rates no single manager could get alone.
- **Body:** Your guest opens the link in your brand and makes their requests. Summit fulfills each one through our Park City vendor network. Because we buy across many homes, we secure rates a single firm can't. Our margin is the gap between those rates and what the guest pays. You are never charged.
- **Closing line:** Nothing to install, no one to hire. You add the link to your pre-arrival flow and we run the rest. A full concierge you offer your guests at no cost to your business.

## Section 4 — What it does for you (three blocks)
- **Eyebrow:** What it does for you
- **Block 1 title:** Better stays, on the record
- **Block 1 body:** The requests that used to slip get handled. It shows up in the reviews, and in what owners hear about how their home is run.
- **Block 2 title:** More repeat and direct bookings
- **Block 2 body:** A guest whose trip was handled comes back to you, and books direct next time instead of through a platform. Those are the most profitable bookings you take.
- **Block 3 title:** Your brand, not ours
- **Block 3 body:** Guests see you, never Summit. We work behind your name, we don't market to your guests, and we don't compete for the next booking. That stays yours.
