# The Summit Eval — does it hold the idea?

Measures **adherence to the vision, not feature-presence.** A surface can work
and still be wrong. This is the bar for "good enough to put in front of a PM or
a guest." Run it before any PM demo, and before merging a guest-facing change.

Two instruments — use both:

1. **Doctrine Scorecard** (fast, principle-by-principle) — below.
2. **Persona pass** (experiential) — 8–10 grounded guest personas react to a
   flow, skewed to the high-spend target ($5–20K concierge spend). A
   budget-conscious guest balking at a fee = **non-target / accepted trade**,
   not a finding. (Proven: this caught the request-box fee/SLA/discretion misses.)

## Scoring

Per surface, per dimension: **PASS · SOFT · FAIL** + a one-line "tell."
- **FAIL** blocks ship/demo.
- **SOFT** needs a written reason or a fix.
- Bar to ship or pitch: **no FAILs; every SOFT justified.**

## The 8 dimensions (with what a violation looks like)

1. **Milk-not-meat** — every revenue line leaves the guest better off, paid
   gladly for real value. *Tell:* it only works if the guest doesn't notice —
   hidden fee, gated value, a default that binds.
2. **Register** — calm, premium, presupposing concierge; "in your pocket," not a
   dashboard. *Tell:* booking-SaaS voice, form-feel, "would you like to…,"
   clutter, copy that's trying too hard.
3. **Menu doctrine (the app is the waiter)** — chef-curated small menu;
   parameters, not solutions. *Tell:* a configurator; asking the guest to pick
   the operational answer (the car, the SKU); endless options.
4. **Channel doctrine** — app is the interface; demand = structured taps; the
   concierge line is the quiet escape, never the primary CTA. *Tell:* "text us"
   as the main action; composing a message where a tap belongs.
5. **Co-brand quiet** — PM brand primary, Summit quiet; rev-share never
   surfaced. *Tell:* Summit shouting; any rev-share / vendor-funded / internal
   cost language a guest or PM can see.
6. **Prime-directive fit** — turns already-happening spend into a remembered
   stay; credit flows to the host. *Tell:* extracting net-new spend the guest
   didn't want; nothing that helps the PM keep the owner.
7. **Effortlessness** — "never pull out your wallet"; fewest taps to done;
   graceful exit always. *Tell:* friction, dead ends, a required field they never
   saw, an open loop with no "what happens next."
8. **Crook test** — offer, never trap; nothing pre-selected. *Tell:* pre-checked
   opt-ins, binding defaults, anything that depends on them not looking.

## Surfaces to run it on

First landing / home (pre-arrival · in-stay · post-stay) · `/plan` · the catalog
· a service wizard · the request box · the tab · a change flow · the
welcome/plan emails. (Ops is internal — exempt from the register dimensions,
but still held to "internal economics never surfaced.")

## Cadence

- Before any PM pitch / demo (the whole guest journey).
- Before merging a guest-facing change (quick scorecard on the touched surface).
- A full persona pass on anything new or reworked.
