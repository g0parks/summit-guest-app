## Findings

**F1 · FAIL (fixed in this PR) — live alcohol strays in the grocery flow.**
Alcohol was pulled 2026-06-25 (Utah control state; Summit cannot supply), but
three guest-facing lines in the ACTIVE grocery wizard still invited it:
"Après · charcuterie, **wine**, fireside things" (stocking option), "Brands you
love, **the wine**, anything to keep clear of" (particulars), and the list
placeholder "…a few good **bottles of red**". Inviting an order we legally
cannot fill. All three rewritten alcohol-free. (The deactivated bar/sommelier
flows keep their copy by deliberate retention — no live path reaches them.)

**F2 · SOFT — wizard copy isn't season-keyed (catalog is).** A July transfer
offers "Ski or snowboard bags"; the concierge card says "powder-day plans"
year-round. The catalog seasons correctly (`staySeason()`), but
`serviceForm.ts` configs and a couple of code-copy lines are static. Fix shape:
either neutral copy ("Gear bags · skis, boards, bikes, clubs") or plumb
`staySeason()` into flow config. Small, worth doing before a summer pilot;
config structure = Parker's call.

**F3 · SOFT — `/plan` isn't signal-aware.** It asked "Should we have a car
waiting when you land?" to a guest with that exact transfer already CONFIRMED.
The cadence emails learned this trick in Track A (PLAN_AHEAD suppression); the
intake should skip or acknowledge already-arranged L1s ("Your car is set").
Roadmap-sized, not pitch-blocking: the double-ask is polite, just not
presupposing-smart.

**F4 · SOFT — `/plan` header shows the Summit wordmark on a co-branded stay.**
The trip app renders "Alpine Crest Collection Concierge, powered by Summit";
`/plan` shows bare SUMMIT. World B (anonymous) has no partner to resolve, but
World A should carry the stay's brand through `resolveBrand()`.

**F5 · SOFT (accepted) — tab lines dated by tab-entry.** A Jul 16 transfer
shows "Mon, Jul 6" on the tab (the day it hit the ledger). Accountant-correct;
a guest may double-take. Cheap fix if wanted: show the service date on lines
tied to a scheduled request.

**F6 · Note (by design) — same-email guests dedup.** Three stays created with
one email resolved to ONE guest ("fill blanks only, never overwrite"). Right
for returning guests; means a spouse booking with the shared email gets greeted
by the first-captured name. PMS feeds send per-booking names — watch it at the
first real PM integration.

**F7 · Note — itinerary tags the airport transfer "In-residence".** Location
default on request-created itinerary items; wrong for transport. One-line-ish
(location by category), cosmetic.
